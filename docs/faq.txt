Q: Who created the character of Sherlock Holmes?
A: Sherlock Holmes was created by Sir Arthur Conan Doyle, a Scottish physician and writer.

Q: What is the profession of Sherlock Holmes?
A: Sherlock Holmes is a consulting detective, renowned for his logical reasoning and observation skills.

Q: Where does Sherlock Holmes live?
A: Sherlock Holmes resides at 221B Baker Street, London.

Q: Who is Sherlock Holmes' close friend and companion?
A: Dr. John H. Watson, a medical doctor and Holmes' biographer, is his close friend and companion.

Q: What is Sherlock Holmes' most famous phrase?
A: Although often misquoted, Holmes is popularly associated with the phrase, 'Elementary, my dear Watson.'

Q: Who is Sherlock Holmes' archenemy?
A: Professor James Moriarty, a criminal mastermind, is considered Holmes' archenemy.

Q: What is the first Sherlock Holmes story?
A: 'A Study in Scarlet,' published in 1887, is the first story featuring Sherlock Holmes.

Q: How many stories are there in the Sherlock Holmes canon?
A: There are 56 short stories and 4 novels in the official Sherlock Holmes canon.

Q: What is the title of the novel featuring the legendary hound?
A: The novel is titled 'The Hound of the Baskervilles,' one of Holmes' most famous cases.

Q: What are some recurring themes in the Sherlock Holmes stories?
A: Themes include the triumph of logic over superstition, the role of science in solving mysteries, and the complexity of human motives.

Q: What is the name of Sherlock Holmes' brother?
A: His brother's name is Mycroft Holmes, who is even more intelligent but less active than Sherlock.

Q: What is the name of Sherlock Holmes' housekeeper?
A: Mrs. Hudson is the housekeeper at 221B Baker Street.

Q: Which novel features the 'Study in Scarlet' clue?
A: 'A Study in Scarlet' features a clue written in blood.

Q: What instrument does Sherlock Holmes play?
A: Holmes plays the violin.

Q: What is the name of the Scotland Yard inspector often working with Holmes?
A: Inspector Lestrade is a recurring Scotland Yard inspector in the stories.

Q: Which story involves the Red-Headed League?
A: 'The Red-Headed League' is one of Holmes' short stories, involving a peculiar job for red-headed men.

Q: What is the name of the woman Holmes admires?
A: Irene Adler, who appears in 'A Scandal in Bohemia,' is the woman Holmes admires.

Q: Which story features the Reichenbach Falls?
A: 'The Final Problem' features Holmes' confrontation with Moriarty at the Reichenbach Falls.

Q: What disguise does Holmes often use?
A: Holmes is known to use a variety of disguises, including a sailor, beggar, and elderly clergyman.

Q: What is the 'Speckled Band' in the story?
A: The 'Speckled Band' refers to a venomous snake used as a murder weapon.

Q: Which story involves the Blue Carbuncle?
A: 'The Adventure of the Blue Carbuncle' involves a stolen gem hidden inside a goose.

Q: What is the main method Holmes uses to solve cases?
A: Holmes relies on observation, deduction, and logical reasoning.

Q: Who narrates most of the Sherlock Holmes stories?
A: Dr. Watson narrates most of the stories, providing a first-hand account.

Q: What is Holmes' attitude towards emotions?
A: Holmes views emotions as distractions that can cloud judgment.

Q: Which story involves the Baskerville family curse?
A: 'The Hound of the Baskervilles' revolves around a curse allegedly haunting the Baskerville family.

Q: What is the title of Holmes' final story?
A: 'His Last Bow' is the final story chronologically in the canon.

Q: What is Holmes' favorite type of hat?
A: Holmes is often depicted wearing a deerstalker hat.

Q: What is Holmes' attitude toward fame?
A: Holmes is largely indifferent to fame and prefers his work to remain private.

Q: Which story involves the Naval Treaty?
A: 'The Adventure of the Naval Treaty' involves a stolen government document.

Q: What is Holmes' opinion of Watson's writing?
A: Holmes often criticizes Watson's writing for being too romanticized.

Q: Which story features the Musgrave Ritual?
A: 'The Adventure of the Musgrave Ritual' involves a centuries-old family tradition hiding a treasure.

Q: What type of tobacco does Holmes prefer?
A: Holmes is known to smoke strong shag tobacco.

Q: Who is the client in 'The Adventure of the Copper Beeches'?

Q: Which story involves a group called the 'Four'?
A: 'The Sign of the Four' involves a treasure and a pact among four convicts.

Q: What is the setting of most Sherlock Holmes stories?
